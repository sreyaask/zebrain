3.3Bookkeeping procedures and transaction recording

  1. Capture. Every bill and receipt is forwarded to a single finance inbox and attached in the accounting software (Zoho Books or Tally are common choices for Indian GST). Recommendation
  2. Code. Assign the account (§3.2), the GST rate and the vendor's GSTIN, and tag the cost to product or overhead.
  3. Reconcile weekly. Match the bank feed and the Razorpay settlement reports to entries. The gateway clearing account (1020) should net to zero after each settlement.
  4. Invoices. Self-serve customers get automatic GST invoices from the gateway or billing system. Business clients get numbered invoices (ZB/2026-27/0001), due in 15 days.
  5. Refunds. Refunds are issued against the original payment, with a credit note referencing the invoice. Failed paid runs caused by Zebrain get GPU-hour credits (account 2300) instead of cash, as the pricing page promises. Verified (pricing copy)
  6. Reimbursements. Claimed monthly with receipts, approved by the budget owner and paid with payroll.
  7. Payroll. Processed monthly with TDS on salaries, PF and professional tax as applicable, using a payroll service.

3.4Revenue recognition and expense categorisation

StreamWhen revenue is recognised
Monthly hosting plansEvenly over the service month. Annual plans are deferred and recognised monthly.
Message top-upsDeferred when sold. Recognised as messages are used, with any remainder recognised on expiry (state an expiry, e.g. 12 months, in the terms).
Paid trainingWhen the run completes and the model is delivered. Prepaid hours not yet used stay deferred. Credits issued for failures are a liability, not revenue.
Business setup feeOn delivery milestones agreed in the SOW (e.g. 50% at a model that passes acceptance tests, 50% at go-live).
Business managed hostingMonthly, as delivered.

Indian accounting standards (AS or Ind AS, depending on company size) govern the details. Confirm the treatment, especially for top-up expiry, with the CA. Confirm

Expense rule of thumb: anything that scales with each unit served (GPU, LLM API, gateway fees, delivery labour) is cost of revenue. Everything else is operating expense. That split is what makes the gross margin meaningful.

3.5Payables, receivables and the closing calendar

Accounts payable

  • Pay vendors on net-15/30 terms, in two weekly payment runs.
  • Check the GSTIN and the bill against the PO or contract before paying.
  • Deduct TDS where applicable and deposit it by the due date.

Accounts receivable

  • Business invoices are due in 15 days.
  • Reminders at day +3, +10 and +20. Service pauses at +30 (stated in the MSA).
  • Track DSO monthly. Target under 30 days.

3.6Pricing, margins and unit economics

Gross margin = (Revenue − Cost of revenue) ÷ Revenue
Contribution margin = Price − variable cost per unit (compute + API + gateway)
CAC = (Sales + marketing spend in period) ÷ new paying customers in period
LTV = (ARPU × gross margin %) ÷ monthly churn  ·  Payback (months) = CAC ÷ (ARPU × GM %)
Break-even revenue = Fixed operating costs ÷ gross margin %

3.9Fundraising, investor reporting and equity

  • Instruments: Indian pre-seed rounds commonly use equity or compulsorily convertible preference shares (CCPS). Valuation, FEMA rules for foreign investors and angel-tax provisions all need a CA or lawyer. Confirm with counsel
  • Cap table hygiene: keep a single source cap table (founders, ESOP pool, investors, fully diluted %). Recommendation Create a 10% ESOP pool before the first priced round, with standard vesting of 4 years and a 1-year cliff.
  • Investor update (monthly, 1 page): headline, KPIs (MRR, models in active use, burn, runway), wins, misses, asks.
  • Data room: incorporation documents, cap table, financials, this manual, product demo, security summary, key contracts.
  • Non-dilutive options: Startup India (DPIIT) recognition and state startup schemes, and cloud credit programmes for GPUs [17].

3.10Financial controls, approval limits and fraud prevention

ControlRule
Approval limitsUnder ₹25k: budget owner · ₹25k–₹2L: CEO · over ₹2L: CEO plus a second signatory · new recurring vendor: CEO
Segregation of dutiesThe person who adds a vendor never releases its payment. Bank payments need maker plus checker in net banking.
Vendor master changesBank-detail changes are confirmed by a phone call to a known number (the most common invoice fraud).
CardsCompany cards per person with monthly limits. Receipts within 7 days.
Cloud spendBilling alerts at 50%, 80% and 100% of budget on every GPU and API account. Hard caps where available.
Refunds and creditsOver ₹5,000 needs CEO approval. All refunds and credits are logged with a reason code.
Audit trailThe accounting system's audit log stays on. Documents are retained for the statutory period (8 years under Indian company law is common; confirm).

3.11Tax and regulatory obligations (India)

Confirm with a CA

This section lists the obligations to discuss with a chartered accountant. It is not tax advice, and rates and thresholds change.

AreaWhat applies, as generally understood
GSTSoftware and SaaS services are generally taxed at 18% GST [5]. Registration depends on turnover thresholds and supply type. Many SaaS companies register early so they can collect GST and claim input credit.
ExportsServices to foreign customers can be zero-rated by filing a Letter of Undertaking (LUT, Form GST RFD-11) each year. Foreign payment must arrive within the prescribed period [5].
Imports of servicesPaying foreign vendors (GPU clouds, Supabase, Vercel, Gemini) may trigger GST under reverse charge for a registered business. Track these bills separately.
TDSDeduct TDS on contractor, professional and rent payments and on salaries, deposit it monthly and file quarterly returns.
Income taxDomestic companies pay corporate tax under the regime they choose. Startup tax holidays need separate approval.
Company lawStatutory auditor appointment, board meetings, annual filings with the Registrar of Companies, director KYC [17].
PayrollProfessional tax (state), PF/ESI once headcount thresholds apply, labour-law registrations (Shops & Establishments).
