1.1Company overview

What it is. Zebrain is a web service that turns a plain-language description ("an assistant that writes PostgreSQL for our sales database") into a fine-tuned small language model owned by the user. Verified The training runs on the user's own Kaggle account as a private GPU script, and the model is published to the user's own Hugging Face account as versions v1, v2… with a model card that shows before-and-after scores [1].

Recommendation

Mission

Give every business and maker a language model of their own that does one job well, answers in their name, and stays theirs.

Vision (2030)

Owning a specialised model is as ordinary as owning a website, and Zebrain is where people make, test, fix and run them.

Recommendation

Values

  • Yours, not ours. Models, data and keys belong to the user. Portability by default.
  • Tested, not hoped. Nothing ships without before/after scores.
  • Plain words. No jargon a shop owner wouldn't use.
  • Honest about limits. Say when a small model can't do something.
  • Frugal by design. Free GPUs first, paid compute when it earns its cost.

Positioning statement

Recommendation

For small businesses and independent builders who want an AI assistant that knows their job and speaks as them, Zebrain is the no-code model maker that trains, tests and publishes a model you own, starting free on your own GPU. Unlike general chat apps such as ChatGPT or Gemini, a Zebrain model is trained on your examples, stays on topic, carries your name, and lives in your account, so you can take it anywhere.

Pricing strategy

  • Free as acquisition: the free tier costs Zebrain almost nothing in compute, because the user's Kaggle quota pays. Kaggle reports around 30 free GPU hours per week per account [3] (third-party sources; confirm on Kaggle).
  • Value metric = messages: customers understand messages, not tokens. Keep tokens internal.
  • Anchor against consumer chat apps: the pricing page compares with ChatGPT Go (₹399), ChatGPT Plus (₹1,999), Google AI Plus (₹399) and Google AI Pro (₹1,950), as checked in October 2026 [12]. Zebrain is not cheaper per message than API models. It wins on ownership, on-brand behaviour and staying on topic, and the page says so honestly.
  • Recommendation Add an annual plan (2 months free) once monthly churn data exists. Do not discount before then.

Decision framework for new features

Priority score = (Reach × Impact × Confidence) ÷ Effort  ·  Reach = users per quarter · Impact 0.25–3 · Confidence 50–100% · Effort = person-weeks

Any feature that touches user keys, user data or model publishing also needs a security review sign-off (§6.4), whatever its score.
