8.1Sales motions

Self-serve (Free → Starter/Pro)

No salesperson involved. The product, onboarding emails and in-app prompts do the selling, and the trigger is usage: "Your free chat closes in 1 hour. Keep it always on for ₹499/month."

Consultative (Business plan)

Founder-led and later Success-led. A discovery call, then a pilot SOW, then a model passing the client's own acceptance questions, then go-live. Price is agreed per client, as the pricing page states. Verified

8.2Lead generation, discovery and qualification

Lead sources: "Book a meeting" on the pricing page (link still a placeholder Verified), inbound from content, agency referrals, and outbound to stores with public FAQ pages.

Qualification (adapted BANT)
QuestionQualified if

Authority: who decides?Owner or ops head on the call
Budget: what do you spend on support today?Can pay the pilot price
Timing: when should it be live?Within 60 days

Discovery call script (20 minutes) Illustrative

  1. Context (3 min): "Tell me how customers reach you today, and what they ask most."
  2. Pain (5 min): "What happens when nobody answers quickly? Have you tried a chatbot? What went wrong?"
  3. Data (4 min): "Where do the right answers live today: FAQ page, a doc, someone's head?"
  4. Show (5 min): the before/after demo on a sample store model, including an off-topic refusal and a correction.
  5. Next step (3 min): "If I send a pilot plan with 25 of your real questions as the test, could we decide by Friday?"

8.4Outreach strategy and email sequence

Outbound sequence for stores Illustrative

Day 1 · Subject: "Your return-policy questions, answered at 2 a.m."
Hi {Name}, I noticed {Store}'s FAQ covers {topic}. We build small AI models that answer those questions in your words and stay on topic. Could I show you one trained on your FAQ, at no cost, in a 15-minute call?

Day 4: a 40-second video of a model answering 3 of their real FAQ questions.
Day 9: case study or test scores from a similar store.
Day 16: "Should I close the loop?" break-up email.

Comply with anti-spam norms: identify the sender, include an opt-out, and only email business addresses published for contact.

8.5Demos, proposals, negotiation and closing

  • Demo rule: always demo on the prospect's own questions (3–5 from their FAQ), with a model trained before the call, never generic examples.
  • Proposal: 2 pages. Problem, pilot scope, the acceptance test (their 25 questions, pass mark agreed upfront), timeline, price, what happens after the pilot.
  • Negotiation: trade scope, not price. A smaller model, fewer languages or a longer timeline lowers the price, while discounts are given only for annual prepayment.
  • Close: the pilot converts to a monthly plan automatically unless the client cancels within 14 days of go-live.
