9.1Company formation and governance

  • Entity: a private limited company incorporated through the MCA's SPICe+ process is the usual vehicle for a venture-funded Indian startup [17]. Assumption Then apply for DPIIT startup recognition.
  • Founders' agreement: roles, vesting (4 years with a 1-year cliff), IP assignment, decision rights, and what happens if a founder leaves.
  • Governance: board meetings as required by law, minutes, a statutory register, and a decision log for one-way doors (§2.3).

9.3Data protection and cybersecurity responsibilities

India's Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Rules were notified on 13 November 2025. They phase in over 18 months, with most substantive obligations (notice, consent, data-principal rights) due from May 2027 [4]. Zebrain is a data fiduciary for account data and should prepare now:

  • Privacy notice in plain language: what is collected (account, keys, run metadata), why, retention (§4.12), processors (Supabase, Gemini, Kaggle, Hugging Face, payments).
  • Consent and withdrawal: sign-up consent, and delete-account that really deletes.
  • Data-principal rights process: access, correction and erasure requests answered within the statutory period.
  • Breach response: notify the Data Protection Board and affected users as the Rules require (Appendix A15). Counsel on timelines.
  • Processor contracts (DPAs) with every vendor that touches user data.
  • Business clients: Zebrain is usually a processor for their end-customers' chats. The MSA must allocate responsibilities.
  • Security baseline: Chapter 4.10 controls, MFA for staff, least privilege, annual penetration test.

9.4Platform terms: Kaggle, Hugging Face, Google

Highest-priority legal question

Zebrain's free tier runs a commercial service's job on users' own Kaggle accounts. Kaggle's terms of use govern how its free compute may be used [15]. Before launch, counsel should review whether this pattern is permitted, what restrictions apply (e.g. to long-running chat sessions), and how Zebrain should describe it to users. Ask Kaggle directly if the opinion is unclear.

  • Hugging Face: users publish to their own accounts with their own tokens. Zebrain uses free static Spaces, because Gradio/Docker Spaces need PRO. Review HF's terms for automated repo creation at scale.
  • Gemini API: follow the additional terms. On unpaid tiers, submitted content may be used to improve Google products [9], so Business data should go through a paid tier.
